KerfPilot Support

KerfPilot helps plan one-dimensional cuts for straight stock and prepare material estimates and customer quotes on iPhone and iPad.

Start with the sample

Open KerfPilot, review the included Window frame sample, and tap Optimize plan. Before cutting, confirm the unit, stock length and price, required piece lengths and quantities, blade kerf, end trims, and reusable-offcut threshold. A complete result means every requested piece fits the displayed plan; it does not guarantee a mathematical global optimum.

Save, move, and recover work

KerfPilot automatically saves valid project edits on the current device. Use Save project to create a .kerfpilot.json backup and Open project to restore one. Use Export CSV and Import CSV for cut-list exchange. Files leave the app only when you choose an export or share action.

If the app reports damaged local project data, export the recovery copy before clearing it. Removing the app or clearing its data can remove local projects, so keep exported backups for important work.

Quotes and PDF

The Quote tab can add labor, other costs, markup or margin, and tax. Use Print quote and choose the iOS print or PDF destination. The customer print layout omits internal material-purchase cost and internal profit.

Troubleshooting

  • If a result is partial, check available stock quantities, stock lengths, trims, kerf, and piece lengths.
  • If an import is rejected, verify that it is a KerfPilot project or supported CSV and that its quantities, units, currency, and numbers are valid.
  • If sharing is cancelled, the project remains unchanged. Retry the export and choose a destination in Files or the share sheet.
  • For a support request, include the KerfPilot version, iOS or iPadOS version, device model, the action attempted, and the exact message shown. Do not attach confidential customer data unless you choose to share it.

KerfPilot is a calculation and recordkeeping aid. Follow project specifications, calibrated equipment, material requirements, and safe work practices.

KerfPilot 支持

KerfPilot 用于在 iPhone 和 iPad 上规划木方、管材、钢筋和型材等直料的一维切割，并计算材料用量和客户报价。

从示例开始

打开 KerfPilot，检查内置的“窗框木方示例”，然后点击“优化方案”。切割前，请确认单位、原料长度与单价、成品长度与数量、锯缝、两端修整量和余料保留下限。结果显示“完整”只表示方案容纳了全部所需成品，不代表数学意义上的全局最优。

保存、转移和恢复项目

KerfPilot 会在当前设备自动保存有效的项目修改。使用“保存项目”生成 .kerfpilot.json 备份，使用“打开项目”恢复；使用“导出 CSV”和“导入 CSV”交换下料清单。只有用户主动选择导出或分享时，文件才会离开应用。

如果应用提示本机项目数据损坏，请先导出恢复副本，再清除它。移除应用或清除应用数据可能删除本机项目，重要工作应保留导出备份。

报价和 PDF

“报价”页可以加入人工费、其他费用、加价率或毛利率和税率。点击“打印报价单”，再选择 iOS 的打印或 PDF 目的地。客户版打印内容不显示内部材料采购成本和内部利润。

故障排查

  • 如果结果为“部分完成”，请检查可用原料数量、原料长度、修整量、锯缝和成品长度。
  • 如果导入被拒绝，请确认文件是 KerfPilot 项目或受支持的 CSV，并检查数量、单位、币种和数字是否合法。
  • 如果取消分享，项目不会改变；可重新导出并在“文件”或系统分享面板选择目的地。
  • 提交支持请求时，请提供 KerfPilot 版本、iOS 或 iPadOS 版本、设备型号、所做操作和屏幕上的完整提示。除非你主动选择，否则不要附带客户敏感数据。

KerfPilot 是计算和记录辅助工具。实际施工仍应遵循项目规范、校准设备、材料要求和安全操作规程。
