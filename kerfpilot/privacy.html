KerfPilot Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

KerfPilot performs cut planning and quote calculations on your iPhone or iPad. The current app does not require an account and does not send project dimensions or quotes to a developer-operated server.

Data collection

The current app and developer do not collect personal data through KerfPilot. The app contains no analytics, advertising, cross-app tracking, account system, or cloud project database.

Data stored on your device

KerfPilot stores project information in local app storage so valid edits can remain available on this device. That information can include project and customer names, notes, stock and piece dimensions, prices, quote inputs. Results are recalculated from the saved inputs when needed. Removing the app or clearing its data can remove local projects.

Files and sharing you control

KerfPilot creates CSV, .kerfpilot.json, and print or PDF output only after you choose the related action. iOS then lets you choose a destination or recipient. Those destinations, recipients, storage providers, and Apple system services handle the file under their own terms. KerfPilot does not automatically upload exported files.

App Store purchase

The App Store download includes all current KerfPilot features. Apple processes the App Store transaction under Apple's policies. KerfPilot does not collect or receive your payment-card details.

Data retention and deletion

Projects remain in local app storage until they are replaced, removed with app data, or the app is uninstalled. Exported copies remain wherever you chose to save or share them. You control those copies through the destination service or device.

Children, tracking, and sale of data

KerfPilot is a general-purpose work utility. It does not knowingly collect children's personal data, track users, sell personal data, or share personal data for targeted advertising.

Changes and questions

If KerfPilot's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy answers must be updated before the changed version is released. Use the KerfPilot support route on this site for privacy questions.

KerfPilot 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 10 日

KerfPilot 在 iPhone 或 iPad 本机完成下料规划和报价计算。当前应用不要求账号，也不会把项目尺寸或报价发送到开发者运营的服务器。

数据收集

当前应用和开发者不会通过 KerfPilot 收集个人数据。应用不包含分析、广告、跨应用跟踪、账号系统或云端项目数据库。

保存在设备上的数据

KerfPilot 使用应用本机存储保存项目信息，使有效修改可以在当前设备继续使用。信息可能包括项目和客户名称、备注、原料与成品尺寸、价格、报价输入；需要时根据保存的输入重新计算结果。移除应用或清除应用数据可能删除本机项目。

由你控制的文件和分享

只有在用户主动选择相应操作后，KerfPilot 才会生成 CSV、.kerfpilot.json 和打印或 PDF 输出。随后由 iOS 让用户选择目的地或接收者。目的地、接收者、存储服务商和 Apple 系统服务按各自条款处理文件。KerfPilot 不会自动上传导出文件。

App Store 购买

App Store 下载已包含 KerfPilot 的全部现有功能。App Store 交易由 Apple 按其政策处理；KerfPilot 不收集也不会收到用户的银行卡详情。

数据保留和删除

项目保留在应用本机存储中，直到被替换、随应用数据一起移除或应用被卸载。导出副本会保留在用户选择的保存或分享位置，用户通过相应设备或目的地服务管理这些副本。

儿童、跟踪和数据出售

KerfPilot 是通用工作工具。应用不会在明知情况下收集儿童个人数据，不跟踪用户，不出售个人数据，也不为定向广告分享个人数据。

变更和问题

如果 KerfPilot 的数据处理方式发生变化，必须在发布变更版本前同步更新本政策和 App Store 隐私回答。隐私问题请使用本站的 KerfPilot 支持页面。
